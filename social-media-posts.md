### LinkedIn
Most people with multiple debts do the same thing: split extra money evenly across all of them.

It feels fair. It's one of the slowest ways to make progress.

The debt snowball method works differently. Pay minimums on everything. Put every extra dollar on your smallest balance until it's gone. Then roll that freed payment into the next debt.

Two things happen fast: one account closes completely, and your payment power grows each month.

It's math and psychology working together.
