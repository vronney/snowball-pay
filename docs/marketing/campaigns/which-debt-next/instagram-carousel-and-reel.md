# Instagram Production Pack: Which Debt Next?

## Carousel format

- Canvas: 1080 x 1350 px, five pages.
- Safe area: keep essential text at least 96 px from every edge.
- Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans style, 600-800 for headings and 400-600 for body.
- Palette: `#f8fafc`, `#ffffff`, `#0f172a`, `#64748b`, `#e2e8f0`; use `#2563eb` only for active emphasis and CTA.
- Layout: left-aligned editorial hierarchy, restrained cards, no gradients or stock-photo finance imagery.

### Page 1 — Hook

Eyebrow: `A CLEARER NEXT PAYMENT`

Headline: `Which debt gets your next extra dollar?`

Subhead: `Use the method you can keep following.`

Visual: three quiet debt rows. Mark one row `FOCUS` in blue without making the whole card blue.

### Page 2 — Snowball

Headline: `Snowball = smallest balance first`

Body: `Best when a quick payoff win helps you stay consistent.`

Example row:

- Store Card
- `$650`
- `24% APR`
- Tag: `FIRST TARGET`

Footer: `Momentum first`

### Page 3 — Avalanche

Headline: `Avalanche = highest APR first`

Body: `Best when minimizing total interest is your top priority.`

Example row:

- Credit Card
- `$3,200`
- `29% APR`
- Tag: `HIGHEST APR`

Footer: `Interest first`

### Page 4 — Comparison

Headline: `The math matters. So does momentum.`

Body: `Compare the payoff order, interest, and debt-free date before you choose.`

Visual:

- Snowball: `smallest balance -> next balance -> final debt`
- Avalanche: `highest APR -> next APR -> final debt`
- A thin timeline or progress line, not a decorative chart.

Headline: `Run your real numbers.`

Body: `See your payoff order and debt-free date with the free SnowballPay calculator.`

Button: `Try the free calculator`

## Caption

If you have more than one debt, “pay extra when you can” leaves out the hardest part:

Where should the extra money go?

The Snowball method starts with the smallest balance. That early win can make the plan easier to keep following.

The Avalanche method starts with the highest APR. That prioritizes reducing interest.

Neither choice should come from guilt or a generic rule. Compare both against your balances, minimums, and real monthly budget.

SnowballPay shows the payoff order and projected debt-free date before you commit to a method.

Save this post for your next payday, then run your numbers with the free calculator. Link in bio.

## Fifteen-second animated Reel

Use the carousel's layout and assets so the Reel feels like the same campaign, not a new design.

| Time | Visual | On-screen copy | Motion | Voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0-2.0s | Three debt rows enter | `Which debt gets the next $100?` | Rows rise in with a 120 ms stagger | “Which debt should get your next extra hundred dollars?” |
| 2.0-5.0s | Smallest balance becomes focus | `SNOWBALL` / `Smallest balance first` | Focus tag settles; other rows dim slightly | “Snowball starts with the smallest balance for an earlier win.” |
| 5.0-8.0s | Highest APR becomes focus | `AVALANCHE` / `Highest APR first` | Blue indicator moves to the high-APR row | “Avalanche starts with the highest rate to reduce interest.” |
| 8.0-11.5s | Two paths appear side by side | `Momentum` / `Interest` | Thin progress lines draw left to right | “The right choice depends on your numbers and what you can keep following.” |
| 11.5-15.0s | CTA card | `Run your real numbers.` | CTA rises once and rests; no pulsing loop | “Compare both free with SnowballPay.” |

### Motion direction

- Use short, functional entrances. Avoid bouncing numbers, spinning icons, or confetti.
- Use 250-400 ms entrances with a gentle ease-out feel.
- Stagger debt rows rather than animating everything at once.
- Keep every frame readable without audio; burn in captions.
- Hold the CTA fully static for the final two seconds.
- Export vertical 1080 x 1920 MP4, 30 fps, approximately 15 seconds.
- Choose calm instrumental audio or publish without music; the voiceover and captions should carry the message.

The Canva connector can generate the static design and story frames, but it does not expose animation or video export controls. Apply this shot list inside Canva after the selected candidate is converted into an editable design.
